## Reduce uncertainty wherever possible

People stay longer when they understand what is happening. Clear labels, useful empty states, predictable navigation, and reassuring feedback all reduce the tiny moments of hesitation that build frustration over time.

Clarity does not need to be loud. It just needs to be consistent.

## Make progress visible

Whether someone is filling out a form, comparing options, or exploring content, visible progress matters. Users feel more confident when the interface helps them sense where they are and what comes next.

That feeling of momentum creates trust. It tells people the product is helping them forward instead of making them work harder.

## Polish is a usability signal

Thoughtful spacing, responsive feedback, and intentional copy do more than make a product look good. They signal care. And care is often what makes users feel safe enough to keep exploring.

Small choices rarely feel dramatic in isolation, but together they influence whether a product feels forgettable or worth returning to.
